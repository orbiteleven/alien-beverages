# Long Haul

## Description
A dockside standard: smoky grain spirit sharpened with brine and a bitter snap at the finish. This is the drink long-run crews order when they clear customs on Ceres, and half the bars on the ring pour it without being asked. The void bloom is barely there — a suggestion of something green and floral under all that smoke. Drink it slow or don't bother.

## Method
1. Add 50ml Ganymede rye analogue, 10ml brine (Callisto), and 10ml kelp syrup to a mixing tin with ice.
2. Add 5ml void bloom and 2 dashes Tannhäuser bitters.
3. Stir until well chilled, about 20 seconds.
4. Strain over a single large cube in a ballast tumbler.
5. Garnish with a kelp ribbon.

## Bartender's notes
The bitters are the house touch — plenty of docks skip them, but without them the finish just trails off like a dead comm channel. If your kelp syrup is running thick from the cold store, loosen it with a splash of warm glacier melt before you build.

Drier: drop the syrup to 5ml and add a third dash of bitters. Sweeter: 15ml syrup and lose the bitters entirely, though at that point you're drinking a different ticket and I'll say so to your face.
