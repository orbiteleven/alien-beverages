# First Shift

## Description
Cold reactor-coil tea over ice, sweetened with glowcap nectar and stretched with glacier melt — the Yard's sunrise drink for people whose sunrise is a wall clock. Bright and herbal with a gentle sweetness, no alcohol, no crash. Apprentices drink it because they're not allowed anything else on shift; half the senior welders drink it because they've seen what the other half look like. It's cheap, fast, and quietly excellent.

## Method
1. Fill a coolant sleeve with ice.
2. Pour 150ml cold reactor-coil tea over.
3. Add 15ml glowcap nectar and 30ml pressed glacier melt.
4. Stir twice, float a glowcap sliver.

## Bartender's notes
Brew the tea the night before and chill it slow — flash-chilled coil tea goes cloudy and bitter, and the whole drink is the tea. The nectar wants to sink; stir from the bottom or the first sip is water and the last is syrup.

That's honestly all there is to it, which is why it never leaves the menu. Sweeter: 20ml nectar. Brighter: a squeeze of Oberon sourfruit turns it into a different, louder drink — good, but warn them.
