# The Foreman's Eye

## Description
Green as an inspection lamp and twice as judgmental: hullmoss distillate stirred with glowcap nectar into something grassy and sweet, then handed a bitter finish that arrives like a clipboard. The name is Yard humor — the foreman's eye finds the flaw, and so does the last sip. It's the thinking crew's mid-shift-adjacent drink, low enough proof to stay respectable, complex enough to be worth slowing down for.

## Method
1. Stir 45ml hullmoss distillate and 15ml glowcap nectar over ice, 20 seconds.
2. Add 25ml pressed glacier melt and 2 dashes Tannhäuser bitters.
3. Stir 10 seconds more, strain into a chilled hexcell glass.
4. Slap the hullmoss sprig against your palm and drop it in.

## Bartender's notes
The slap isn't showmanship — hullmoss holds its aroma tight and needs the bruise. Fresh sprigs only; a limp garnish on this drink is a limp drink. The melt in step 2 is doing balance work, softening the distillate's edge so the bitters can land clean.

Sweeter: 20ml nectar and it goes friendly, loses a little authority. Drier: 10ml, third dash of bitters, and now it's the foreman on a bad day. Void bloom distillate substitutes into something floral and much less funny.
