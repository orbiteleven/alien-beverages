# Hazard Pay

## Description
Strong, hot, and smoky in roughly that order — the drink you earn, not the drink you want. Kessler still-run is rough off the line, and the smelter bars lean into it: pepper oil for heat, vent resin for smoke, burnt sugar so the finish forgives you. It's named for the line item that pays for it, and the regulars drink it the night hazard pay clears and mostly not otherwise. One per customer is a house rule in at least two establishments.

## Method
1. Stir a bar spoon of burnt ballast sugar into 55ml Kessler still-run synthol until dissolved.
2. Add ice, 2 drops Deimos pepper oil, and a shaved sliver of smoked vent resin.
3. Stir a full minute — the resin needs time to give anything up.
4. Strain into a salvage tin over one big cube. Drop a resin shard on the cube.

## Bartender's notes
Kessler batches drift; smell the bottle before you pour. A batch that smells like solvent needs a longer stir and an extra pinch of sugar — you're rounding corners, not hiding them; nothing hides Kessler.

If someone asks for it "less angry," swap the still-run for triple-scrubbed vat ethanol and keep everything else — smoother ride, same weather. Don't cut the pepper oil below two drops; without heat this is just sweet smoke, and there are better tickets for that.
