# Ten-Percent Gravity

## Description
The Slipway's low-grav party trick: two densities of hydrofoam layered over charged melt so the whole drink is mostly air, holding its strata in Luna's gentle pull like a science exhibit that got out. It's barely alcoholic, airy and sweet, and it collapses into fizz as you drink it — which is the drink working, not failing. Tourists photograph it; regulars order it for tourists and then quietly get one for themselves.

## Method
1. Pour 60ml carbonated glacier melt into a zero-G bulb.
2. Stir in 10ml glowcap nectar, gently.
3. Charge 30ml reclaimed hydrofoam and layer it on with the bulb tilted.
4. Charge 30ml sweet hydrofoam and float it on top of that — slower this time.
5. Seal. Serve within two minutes or serve a memory.

## Bartender's notes
Layer order is density order, and density depends on your whipper pressure — calibrate on a spare bulb at the start of shift, because mid-service is a bad time to learn your reclaimed foam is running heavy. The two-minute clock is real. Build it when the guest is seated, not when they order.

Off Luna this drink mostly doesn't work; under full gravity the layers shear in seconds and you get sweet fog. If you must, halve the melt and serve it as a short pour. Less sweet: skip the nectar; the sweet foam carries plenty.
