# Night Cycle

## Description
When Meridian dims the corridor lights for night cycle, the bars switch from brown spirits to this: grassy hullmoss distillate shaken bright with sourfruit and capped with a stiff head of reclaimed hydrofoam. It's tart and green and a little weird, like most things on Meridian. Transit crews between connections drink it because it's refreshing without being strong enough to miss a boarding call. The foam holds the aroma; drink through it, don't stir it in.

## Method
1. Shake 40ml hullmoss distillate, 20ml Oberon sourfruit juice, and 15ml 1:1 kelp syrup hard with ice.
2. Double-strain into a pressure flute.
3. Charge 20ml reclaimed hydrofoam through the whipper and float it on top.
4. Express the sourfruit twist over the foam and perch it on the rim.

## Bartender's notes
Reclaimed hydrofoam gets a look from tourists — tell them it's triple-filtered and change the subject. The foam is doing real work: hullmoss aroma is volatile and the cap traps it. A flat Night Cycle tastes like lawn clippings; a foamed one tastes like a greenhouse.

No sourfruit? Don't fake it with cordial — the acid is the spine. Drier: 10ml syrup, and the grass gets louder. Sweeter: 20ml, tourist spec, fine, whatever, it's your bar tab.
