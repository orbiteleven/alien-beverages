# Static Cling

## Description
The Underdeck's low-proof crowd-pleaser: brine-algae ferment turned bright and fizzy-feeling with sourfruit, a crackling sugar rim, and a cap of hydrofoam that clings to the glass the whole way down — hence the name. It drinks tart and saline with a light sweetness, more refreshing than its ingredients have any right to be. Salvage crews order rounds of them because you can still run a cutting torch afterward. The rim genuinely crackles; children of dock workers dare each other to lick it.

## Method
1. Rim a hexcell glass with static sugar.
2. Shake 60ml Enceladus brine-algae ferment, 20ml Oberon sourfruit juice, and 10ml static sugar syrup with ice.
3. Strain into the rimmed glass over fresh ice.
4. Top with 15ml reclaimed hydrofoam.

## Bartender's notes
The ferment does the heavy lifting, so the fresher the bottle the better the drink — a tired ferment makes a Static Cling that tastes like licking a dock railing. The foam should sit proud of the rim by a finger; skimp and the drink looks as cheap as it is.

Sweeter: bump the syrup to 15ml, the rim covers the rest. Drier: drop the syrup and let the sourfruit and brine slug it out — sharper, arguably more Underdeck. Don't bother with fancy ferments here; this is a volume ticket and it knows it.
