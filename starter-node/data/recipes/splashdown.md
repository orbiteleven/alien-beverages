# Splashdown

## Description
The lightest thing the Underdeck serves with a straight face: brine-algae ferment stretched long with carbonated melt and brightened with sourfruit, saline and tart with a busy fizz. It drinks like sea spray, if any of its drinkers had seen a sea. Salvage divers order it after tank work — low proof, high refreshment, salty enough to replace what the suit took. The name is aspirational; nothing on Pallas has ever splashed down anywhere.

## Method
1. Fill a pressure flute halfway with ice.
2. Add 50ml Enceladus brine-algae ferment and 15ml Oberon sourfruit juice.
3. Top with 80ml carbonated glacier melt.
4. One gentle lift with the spoon; wheel on the rim.

## Bartender's notes
This is a two-minute ticket and it should stay one — the only way to ruin it is overthinking. Keep the ferment cold and the melt freshly charged; both go dull fast, and a dull Splashdown is salty soda.

If someone wants it sweeter, 10ml glowcap nectar folds in without an argument. A dash of saltgrass tincture makes the "diver's spec" — saltier still, borderline electrolyte drink, and the tank crews swear by it with the fervor of people who bill by the hour underwater.
