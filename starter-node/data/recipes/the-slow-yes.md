# The Slow Yes

## Description
Named for how Europans negotiate — the answer that takes an evening to arrive and can't be rushed. Cask-aged kelpwine sweetened with glowcap nectar and steadied with bitters, mellow and earthy with a honeyed middle. It's a talking drink: low enough proof to keep the conversation going, interesting enough to fill the silences. Brinehouse tradition says you don't finalize a deal until the second round of these, so the bar pours them in pairs by default.

## Method
1. Stir 80ml cask-aged kelpwine and 12ml glowcap nectar over ice, 25 seconds.
2. Add 2 dashes Tannhäuser bitters.
3. Stir 10 seconds more and strain into a salvage tin over one large cube.
4. Float a glowcap sliver.

## Bartender's notes
Cask character carries this drink, so pour your best-behaved barrel — you want round and nutty, not the sharp young stuff. The bitters keep the nectar from getting sleepy; without them the whole thing goes soft by the third sip.

Sweeter: 16ml nectar, and it becomes dessert-adjacent — fine for the right table. Drier: 8ml and a third dash of bitters, which the older negotiators prefer, possibly as a bargaining posture. Serve warm-ish (cool room, not chilled) if the tin's been sitting cold; this one dies below cellar temperature.
