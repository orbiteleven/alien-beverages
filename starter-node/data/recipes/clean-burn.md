# Clean Burn

## Description
Zero alcohol, zero apology — glacier melt two ways, sharpened with sourfruit and a little rimefrost sweetness, fizzing like it has somewhere to be. "Clean burn" is what the Elevator techs call a perfect engine cycle, and the drink is named in that spirit: crisp, tart, nothing wasted. It's the standing order for anyone on duty, on call, or on their last credit. Most of the bar can't tell it apart from the hard tickets at a glance, which is precisely the point.

## Method
1. Chill a pressure flute.
2. Add 30ml pressed glacier melt, 20ml Oberon sourfruit juice, and 12ml rimefrost cordial.
3. Top with 90ml carbonated glacier melt down the side.
4. Hang the dehydrated sourfruit wheel on the rim.

## Bartender's notes
Treat it with the same respect as the charged tickets — cold glass, gentle build, no shortcuts — because the on-duty crowd notices condescension faster than they notice flavor. The still melt in step 2 isn't filler; all-carbonated builds go harsh and prickly.

Sweeter: 18ml cordial. Drier: skip the cordial and double the sourfruit — that version bites, and the dock pilots love it. If someone wants it "with a little something," a dash of Ionian saltgrass tincture keeps it interesting without breaking its promise.
