# Ballast Burn

## Description
A smelter-row special: clean vat spirit set on fire with pepper oil and rounded out by sugar burnt black on a hot plate. It hits hot, then salty, then sweet, in that order, every time. Furnace crews drink it standing up between pours of actual molten metal, which tells you everything about the clientele. Not a sipping drink — a punctuation mark.

## Method
1. Rim a shockglass with vent salt and set aside.
2. Shake 45ml triple-scrubbed vat ethanol, 10ml Callisto brine, and 15ml burnt ballast sugar syrup hard with ice.
3. Add 3 drops Deimos pepper oil directly to the tin and shake twice more.
4. Strain into the rimmed shockglass, no ice.

## Bartender's notes
Three drops of pepper oil, not four. The oil blooms in the alcohol and a fourth drop takes it from warm to weapon — I've watched dockhands twice your size tap out. If the burnt sugar's gone to hard candy in the jar, remelt it with a splash of glacier melt; don't chip it in solid.

Drier version doesn't really exist — the sugar is structural. For a gentler ride, halve the pepper oil and add 10ml more brine; the salt carries what the heat was doing.
