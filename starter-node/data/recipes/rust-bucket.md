# Rust Bucket

## Description
Named for the ships, not the glass — though the Underdeck serves it in an actual salvage tin, so take your pick. Red kelpwine gives it a tart, winey backbone; the glowcap nectar sweetens without smoothing, and the rustleaf tincture leaves a faint metallic tang that regulars swear is the whole personality. It's cheap, it's fast, and it tastes like payday two days before payday. Scrappers order them in pairs.

## Method
1. Fill a salvage tin with crushed ice.
2. Pour in 90ml red-vat kelpwine and 10ml brine (Callisto).
3. Add 15ml glowcap nectar and 2 dashes rustleaf tincture.
4. Give it one lazy stir with whatever's clean.
5. Wedge the dehydrated sourfruit wheel onto the rim.

## Bartender's notes
Kelpwine varies barrel to barrel — taste before you build. A sour barrel needs 20ml nectar; a flabby one needs an extra dash of rustleaf to wake it up. If someone brings back a Rust Bucket saying it tastes like blood, that's the tincture doing its job; if it tastes like a battery, you over-dashed.

No real substitutions here because there's nothing precious to protect. Out of glowcap nectar, any sweet syrup survives. Out of kelpwine, sell them something else — this drink is the wine, full stop.
