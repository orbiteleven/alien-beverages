# Coolant Leak

## Description
Pale green, ice cold, and fizzing gently in a coolant sleeve — the visual joke writes itself and the relay techs never tire of it. Underneath the bit, it's a genuinely refreshing long drink: Synthline lengthened with carbonated glacier melt, sweetened with glowcap nectar until it glows faintly gold-green in the right light. Sweet, icy, effervescent, zero menace. The one drink on the relay menu you can order without a story attached.

## Method
1. Chill a coolant sleeve until it fogs.
2. Add 40ml Synthline 40, 15ml glowcap nectar, and 10ml rimefrost cordial.
3. Top with 100ml carbonated glacier melt, poured down the inside wall.
4. One gentle stir, then float the glowcap sliver.

## Bartender's notes
Nectar first, bubbles last, always — carbonated melt poured onto undissolved nectar leaves a sweet sludge at the bottom and a tart lie on top. The glow is real but faint; kill the bar lights over the well when you serve one and you'll sell four more.

Sweeter is easy (20ml nectar), drier is easy (skip the cordial), and it survives both, which is why it's the relay's training-wheels ticket. Just keep everything brutally cold. A warm Coolant Leak is exactly as appetizing as the name suggests.
