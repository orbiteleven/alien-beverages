# Second Airlock

## Description
"Always cycle the second airlock" is the safety poster; the drink is what you have after you did. Clean vat spirit shaken with sourfruit, rimefrost, and a pinch of static sugar — tart, cold, and completely without subtext. It's the Tug Pilot's neater sibling: same speed, better manners. Meridian pours hundreds of these a cycle and nobody has ever sent one back, which around here counts as a miracle.

## Method
1. Shake 50ml triple-scrubbed vat ethanol, 20ml Oberon sourfruit juice, 10ml rimefrost cordial, and a pinch of static sugar with ice.
2. Shake until the tin frosts.
3. Double-strain into a chilled shockglass.
4. Hang a dehydrated sourfruit wheel on the rim.

## Bartender's notes
The pinch of sugar isn't for sweetness, it's for texture — it rounds the ethanol's edges so the cordial doesn't have to. Skip it and the drink turns thin and slightly mean. This is a good bottle-test drink: any harshness you taste is your vat spirit, not the build.

Sweeter: 15ml cordial. Drier: drop the cordial to 5ml and add a splash more sourfruit. It also batches beautifully — five builds in one tin for a docking crew, and they'll remember you at tip time.
