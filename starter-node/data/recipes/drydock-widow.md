# Drydock Widow

## Description
The strongest and smokiest pour on the smelter row, stacked smoke on smoke — charred grain spirit and vent resin — with pepper heat over a dark sweet undertow. It's named for the bars that ring every drydock, which collect the crews of ships that aren't going anywhere for a while. Nobody drinks Widows quickly and nobody drinks them often. The first sip is a wall; the third is a fireplace; there should not be a fifth.

## Method
1. Muddle a bar spoon of burnt ballast sugar with 10ml warm glacier melt in a mixing glass.
2. Shave in a sliver of smoked vent resin and stir dry, 20 seconds.
3. Add 60ml smoked dockgrain spirit, ice, and 2 drops Deimos pepper oil.
4. Stir a slow 45 seconds and strain over one large cube in a ballast tumbler.
5. Rest a resin shard on the cube.

## Bartender's notes
Two smokes is a lot of smoke — the sugar isn't optional and neither is the long stir; both are pulling this drink back from the ledge. If your resin batch runs bitter, drop the pepper to one drop; bitterness plus heat compounds into something punitive.

There is no lighter version, and the row's bartenders have a pact about it: the Widow is the Widow. Send the curious to a Deadhead Run — same neighborhood, survivable rent. And check your pours; at this strength a heavy hand is a safety issue, not generosity.
