# The Custody Transfer

## Description
A custody transfer is the moment cargo legally changes hands, and this is the drink both parties take after the manifests match: distilled thresher kelp thrown long with brine-algae ferment until it turns silky, seasoned with saltgrass, dry and savory and deeply maritime. It tastes like the Brinehouse smells, in a good way. Two glasses, one throw, split down the middle — tradition says whoever pours keeps the pearl.

## Method
1. Combine 45ml distilled thresher kelp and 30ml Enceladus brine-algae ferment in a tin with ice.
2. Add 1 dash Ionian saltgrass tincture.
3. Throw between tins eight times, high and slow — the aeration knits the two ferments together.
4. Strain into a ballast tumbler over one cube and drop in a frozen brine pearl.

## Bartender's notes
The throw is doing chemistry, not theater: unthrown, the kelp distillate and the ferment sit apart like strangers, and the drink tastes like two half-drinks. Eight passes minimum. Your arm will learn.

There's no sweet adjustment and requests for one should be met with a Mudfoot. For a drier, sharper transfer, add a second dash of saltgrass and skip the pearl — dock lawyers order it that way, which tells you something about dock lawyers.
