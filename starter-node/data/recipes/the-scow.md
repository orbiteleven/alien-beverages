# The Scow

## Description
Half wine, half roasted duneseed brew, seasoned with saltgrass — the Brinehouse's answer to wanting a drink and a hot meal when you can only afford one. It's earthy and saline and unglamorous, served in a dock jar with duneseed dust across the top like it's proud of itself. Barge crews (the actual scow pilots) drink it at shift change, both directions. It tastes better than it reads, which is also true of the barges.

## Method
1. Fill a dock jar halfway with warm roasted duneseed brew, about 90ml.
2. Add 90ml red-vat kelpwine.
3. Add 1 dash Ionian saltgrass tincture and stir once.
4. Dust the top with roasted duneseed.

## Bartender's notes
Serve it warm, not hot — hot kelpwine goes sour-smelling and you'll hear about it. The proportions are forgiving; the Brinehouse pours it by eye and so should you. One dash of saltgrass, though. It's a seasoning, and two dashes makes the whole jar taste like harbor water.

There's no upscale version and attempts have been made. If someone wants it fancier, add 10ml glowcap nectar and don't tell the barge crews. Drier: more brew, less wine — at 2:1 it's practically breakfast.
