# Last Tether

## Description
The drink they pour at the Slipway when someone's shipping out on a long contract — one for the road, floral and cold and just salty enough to sting. Void bloom distillate barely sweetened with rimefrost, a saline edge from the saltgrass, and a brine pearl that dissolves as slowly as goodbyes do. Austere is the polite word; the regulars call it "the handshake." Nobody orders a second.

## Method
1. Combine 50ml void bloom distillate and 10ml rimefrost cordial in a chilling coil.
2. Add 2 dashes Ionian saltgrass tincture.
3. Flash-chill to near-freezing and pour into a zero-G bulb.
4. Drop in one frozen brine pearl and seal the bulb.

## Bartender's notes
Keep the cordial light-handed — this drink is supposed to be a little severe, and sweetness dulls the sting that gives it meaning. The pearl is doing the long work; by the last pull the drink is saltier than the first, which is the whole poem.

If the guest wants something friendlier, steer them to a Vacuum Bloom — same flowers, kinder weather. Substituting hullmoss distillate makes it grassy and wrong. Drier: skip the cordial entirely; it's brutal and correct and I'll pour it for you exactly once.
