# Deadhead Run

## Description
A deadhead run is flying empty — all fuel cost, no cargo — and this drink is for the pilots stuck doing it. Smoked dockgrain shaken hard with burnt sugar and a thread of pepper heat, served over a salt rim that keeps every sip honest. It tastes like smoke and caramel with a slow burn behind, and it does its job without conversation. The smelter row pours it strong because the deadhead crowd has nowhere to be.

## Method
1. Rim a salvage tin with vent salt.
2. Shake 50ml smoked dockgrain spirit and 15ml burnt ballast sugar syrup with ice.
3. Add 2 drops Deimos pepper oil, one short second shake.
4. Strain into the tin over fresh ice.

## Bartender's notes
Shaking a smoked spirit is heresy in some bars — do it anyway; the aeration softens the char and the sugar needs the agitation. Keep the pepper oil to two drops; deadheading pilots order these in multiples and heat compounds faster than they think.

Out of burnt sugar, plain static sugar works but the drink loses its bottom register — add a sliver of vent resin to the shake to fake the depth. Drier: 10ml sugar and a heavier salt rim. Sweeter: 20ml, and skip the second drink.
