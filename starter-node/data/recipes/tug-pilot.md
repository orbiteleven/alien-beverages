# Tug Pilot

## Description
Three ingredients, no waiting — like the job. Dockgrain spirit shaken with sourfruit and just enough static sugar to keep the peace, finishing dry and a little rough, the way tug pilots like most things. It's Meridian's default "a drink, whatever's fast" answer, and it has probably ended more shifts than the station klaxon. Tart up front, bright in the middle, and a grain-dry landing.

## Method
1. Shake 50ml Ceres dockgrain spirit, 25ml Oberon sourfruit juice, and one bar spoon of static sugar with ice until cold.
2. Double-strain into a chilled shockglass.
3. Garnish with a sourfruit twist.

## Bartender's notes
Dissolve the sugar in the juice before the spirit goes in — dockgrain is cold-blooded and the sugar will sulk at the bottom otherwise. Shake it properly; under-diluted dockgrain has a burr that the sourfruit can't sand off alone.

This is the template drink for half the menu, so it takes substitutions gracefully: Vesta pale for a cleaner ride, smoked dockgrain for a campfire version that has its own small cult. Sweeter: two spoons of sugar. Drier: none at all — that one's called a "hard tow" and it's exactly what it sounds like.
