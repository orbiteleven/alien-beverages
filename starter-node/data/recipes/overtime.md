# Overtime

## Description
The Graveyard Shift's quieter cousin: no smoke, just warm dockgrain spirit and reactor-coil tea sweetened with glowcap nectar, with a bitter thread to keep you honest and a dusting of roasted duneseed on top. It tastes like the second half of a double you didn't volunteer for. Yard crews drink it when they want the warmth without the ceremony. Goes down easy, which is either the point or the problem.

## Method
1. Heat 130ml cold reactor-coil tea to steaming.
2. In a warmed coolant sleeve, combine 35ml Ceres dockgrain spirit and 15ml glowcap nectar.
3. Top with the hot tea, add 2 dashes Tannhäuser bitters.
4. Dust the surface with roasted duneseed.

## Bartender's notes
The duneseed dust isn't garnish-optional — it toasts the aroma and the drink reads flat without it. Grind it fresh if you can; pre-ground goes stale in a week and smells like cardboard.

For the third-shift crowd who'll order two, drop the spirit to 25ml and nobody has to know. Sweeter: 20ml nectar, fine. Drier: swap the nectar for 10ml kelp syrup and add a dash of bitters — darker, less friendly, arguably better.
