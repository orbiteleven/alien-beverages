# The Long Haul (Dock 7 Pour)

## Description
The house version of the drink every dock on Ceres claims to have invented: same smoke, same salt, but thrown between tins to aerate and finished with a shard of smoked vent resin that keeps dissolving as you drink. The result is softer up front and longer at the back, floral in the middle where the standard pour is just smoke. Regulars say the last sip is the whole point. Order it when the ship's unloaded and nobody's waiting on you.

## Method
1. Combine 50ml Ganymede rye analogue, 10ml Callisto-grade brine, and 15ml 1:1 kelp syrup in the first tin.
2. Add 4 drops voidbloom extract.
3. Throw the mix between two tins six times, long pours, over a strainer.
4. Pour into a ballast tumbler over one large cube.
5. Drop in a small resin shard and lay the charred kelp ribbon across the rim.

## Bartender's notes
The throw matters — shaking bruises the extract and stirring leaves it sitting on top like a slick. Six passes, long arcs, no shortcuts. If your voidbloom extract has gone amber, it's oxidized; use half the drops or the perfume turns to soap.

Resin shards vary by batch. Taste one before service — a bitter batch means you shave the shard thin, or the last third of the drink goes acrid. Drier palate: cut syrup to 10ml. Sweeter: bigger resin shard, not more syrup.
