# Dockhand's Breakfast

## Description
Hot roasted duneseed brew under a thick cap of sweet hydrofoam, with burnt sugar stirred through the bottom — no alcohol, all ballast. This is what Dock 7 actually runs on: the morning shift lines up for it before the cranes power on, and the bar sells more of these before 0700 than everything else combined. Roasted, sweet, creamy, and hot enough to hold with gloves on. It is, functionally, breakfast.

## Method
1. Stir a bar spoon of burnt ballast sugar into 200ml hot roasted duneseed brew until dissolved.
2. Pour into a warmed dock jar.
3. Spoon 30ml sweet hydrofoam over the top.
4. Dust with roasted duneseed.

## Bartender's notes
Brew it strong — the hydrofoam mutes everything under it, and a weak Breakfast tastes like warm milkwater with opinions. The burnt sugar goes in the brew, never the foam; sugar on foam slides off and pools like an accusation.

For the crew coming off night shift rather than heading in, a 30ml pour of dockgrain spirit turns it into an Overtime cousin — charge them accordingly. Less sweet: half the sugar, the roast carries it. There's no dairy-free complaint to field; there was never any dairy.
