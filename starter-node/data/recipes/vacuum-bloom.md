# Vacuum Bloom

## Description
The Slipway's showpiece: void bloom two ways — distilled for structure, cold-pressed for perfume — flash-chilled until it pours with almost no viscosity at all. It tastes like a flower garden that's been thoroughly disinfected, in the best possible way: floral, crisp, barely sweet, gone before you've finished deciding about it. Spacers buy it for people they're trying to impress. It works more often than it should.

## Method
1. Combine 45ml void bloom distillate, 8ml cold-pressed void bloom, and 15ml rimefrost cordial in a chilling coil.
2. Add 20ml pressed glacier melt.
3. Flash-chill to just above freezing — under a minute in the coil; do not let it slush.
4. Pour into a zero-G bulb and seal.
5. Float a single void bloom petal in the neck of the bulb.

## Bartender's notes
Temperature is the entire drink. Served merely cold it's perfume soup; flash-chilled it snaps shut and turns crisp. If you don't have a coil, stir it over salted ice twice as long as feels reasonable, and accept it'll be 90% as good.

Cold-pressed void bloom fades fast once opened — a week, maybe. Old stock reads as soap, and there's no fixing it downstream. Drier: cut the cordial to 10ml. Sweeter: don't add syrup, add 5ml more cold-press; the sweetness you're wanting is actually aroma.
