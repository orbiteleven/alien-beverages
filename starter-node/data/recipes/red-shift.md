# Red-Shift

## Description
Red kelpwine lengthened with carbonated melt, sweetened with burnt sugar, and finished with a single bead of pepper oil that spreads across the surface like a warning light. Spicy, sweet, fizzy — a low-proof drink with a loud personality. The Underdeck pours it for the early crowd, when people want flavor more than force. The pepper arrives on the second sip and stays exactly one sip too long, which is the joke.

## Method
1. Fill a hexcell glass with ice.
2. Stir 15ml burnt ballast sugar syrup into 80ml red-vat kelpwine and pour over.
3. Top with 60ml carbonated glacier melt.
4. Drop one bead of Deimos pepper oil dead center of the surface.
5. Serve without stirring — the drinker breaks the bead themselves.

## Bartender's notes
One bead. It's a garnish with a fuse, and stirring it in turns a wink into an assault. If the bar's out of a dropper, dip a skewer tip in the oil and touch the surface — less precise, same effect.

Sweeter: 20ml syrup, the pepper forgives it. Less spicy is simple — no bead — but rename it on the ticket or the regulars will send it back on principle. Flat carbonated melt kills this drink dead; check your charge.
