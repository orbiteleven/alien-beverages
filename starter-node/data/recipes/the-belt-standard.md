# The Belt Standard

## Description
Every relay in the Anser Belt pours this and every relay pours it the same, which out here is practically a constitutional achievement. Pale Vesta grain, glowcap nectar, sourfruit — bright, sweet-tart, smooth enough for anyone. It's the drink you order in a bar you've never been to when you don't want to learn anything about the bartender. Reliable as station time, and the Belt does not say that about much.

## Method
1. Shake 50ml Vesta pale grain analogue, 20ml Oberon sourfruit juice, and 15ml glowcap nectar with ice until well chilled.
2. Double-strain into a chilled shockglass.
3. Express the sourfruit twist over the surface and drop it in.

## Bartender's notes
The whole point is consistency, so measure — this is not a by-eye ticket, and a Standard that varies is a Standard that fails. Fresh sourfruit if you have it; concentrate runs sweet, so pull the nectar back to 10ml to compensate.

It takes flavors politely if a guest wants to wander: a dash of Tannhäuser dries the finish, a bar spoon of kelp-forward anything makes it a Belt Nonstandard and better than it sounds. Sweeter: 20ml nectar. Drier: 10ml. It'll take all of it without complaint. That's the job.
