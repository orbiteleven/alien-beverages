# Dead Reckoning

## Description
Navigation without instruments; a drink without a net. Raw Kessler still-run tamed — barely — by long stirring, then pointed in a direction by bitters and a saline thread of saltgrass. Austere is underselling it. This is the strongest regular pour on the relay menu, drunk by long-haul navigators the night before a burn, one each, like a superstition. It tastes like resolve and slightly like the inside of a fuel line, and its drinkers would not change either.

## Method
1. Add 60ml Kessler still-run synthol to a mixing glass over dense, cold ice.
2. Stir a full 60 seconds — dilution is the only mercy this drink gets.
3. Add 3 dashes Tannhäuser bitters and 1 dash Ionian saltgrass tincture.
4. Stir 15 seconds more, strain into a chilled ballast tumbler, no ice.
5. Garnish with a candied hullmoss sprig — the one sweet thing on the horizon.

## Bartender's notes
The long stir is load-bearing. Kessler under-diluted is a solvent with a garnish, and over-diluted it's dishwater — sixty seconds over good dense ice hits the seam. If your ice is wet and fast-melting, cut to forty.

No substitutions worth listing; the drink is the Kessler, faults included. A guest who wants it gentler should be steered to a Stevedore and a guest who wants it stronger should be steered to a bunk.
