# The Quartermaster

## Description
Dry, smoky, bitter, and exactly measured — like the man it's named for, who allegedly weighed the resin shards. Pale Vesta grain gives it a clean spine; the vent resin and Tannhäuser do all the talking. No sugar anywhere in the build, which regulars mention with either pride or a wince. It's the drink you order when you want the bar to know you're not new here.

## Method
1. Shave a thin sliver of smoked vent resin into a mixing glass.
2. Add 60ml Vesta pale grain analogue and stir dry for 30 seconds to loosen the resin.
3. Add ice and 3 dashes Tannhäuser bitters.
4. Stir 40 seconds and strain into a chilled ballast tumbler, no ice.
5. Garnish with a resin shard balanced on the rim.

## Bartender's notes
The dry pre-stir is not optional — resin barely dissolves once the spirit is cold, and skipping it gets you smoky perfume floating on nothing. Shave thin. A fat sliver in a sugarless drink turns medicinal by the halfway mark.

There's no sweet version; the moment sugar enters, you're making a bad Hazard Pay. If a guest finds it too stern, one bar spoon of glacier melt in the stir opens it up considerably — the Quartermaster himself would have logged that as waste, but he's not on shift.
