# Airlock Kiss

## Description
Named for the puff of frost you get when two ships mate airlocks in vacuum — a small, cold, weirdly tender thing, and so is the drink. Synthline flash-chilled with rimefrost and glowcap nectar, sweet and delicate, served sealed in a bulb still fogged from the coil. The Slipway sells it to couples and to people drinking alone with equal ceremony. It's gone in six sips and leaves a faint sweetness like the memory of one.

## Method
1. Combine 45ml Synthline 40, 12ml rimefrost cordial, and 10ml glowcap nectar in a chilling coil.
2. Flash-chill until the mix fogs the line.
3. Pour into a zero-G bulb, drop in a frozen brine pearl, and seal while cold.
4. Serve fogged — never wipe the bulb.

## Bartender's notes
The fog is the garnish, functionally — wipe the bulb and you've undressed the drink. Nectar and cordial together run sweet, so measure honestly; a heavy hand makes it cloying and the delicacy is the sale.

For a drier kiss, halve the nectar and let the pearl's salt lean in early. Substituting white synthol works fine; substituting anything with character does not — this drink is a mood, not a flavor showcase, and moods bruise easy.
