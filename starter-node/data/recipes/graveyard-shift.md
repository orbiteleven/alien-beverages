# Graveyard Shift

## Description
The hot drink of the Kepler Yard night crews: smoked grain spirit lengthened with warm reactor-coil tea and sweetened with kelp syrup until it stops fighting you. It goes down like a campfire someone apologized for. Welders order it at 0300 station time and hold the sleeve with both hands. Sweet, smoky, and warm enough to matter — a drink that's half meal, half excuse to stand still.

## Method
1. Warm a coolant sleeve with hot water and dump it.
2. Heat 120ml cold reactor-coil tea to steaming — never boiling.
3. In the sleeve, combine 40ml smoked dockgrain spirit and 20ml thresher kelp syrup.
4. Top with the hot tea and add 2 dashes Tannhäuser bitters.
5. Stir once, garnish with a charred kelp ribbon laid over the top.

## Bartender's notes
Boiled tea goes tannic and the smoke turns to ash — steaming, not rolling. If your syrup is fresh-batch and thin, add it after the tea so it doesn't vanish into the heat. The bitters keep the sweetness honest; skip them and it's dessert.

For a drier build, 10ml syrup and a third dash of bitters, and warn them it'll taste like the yard smells. Sweeter, go 25ml, no more — past that the smoke gives up. Unsmoked dockgrain works if that's the shelf you've got, but then it's just hot tea with opinions.
